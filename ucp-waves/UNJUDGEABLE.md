# UNJUDGEABLE — claim classes no fact sheet can settle

**Named in advance: 2026-11-06 · PHASE 0b, UCP wave program**
**Function:** the structural NEEDS-VERIFY population for theuniversalcommerceprotocol.com. Every claim in these classes is marked NEEDS-VERIFY in waves regardless of how confidently a page states it — not because the claim is false, but because no primary source available to this programme can judge it. Named before the waves fire so verdicts stay mechanical (TM wave-23 pattern).

## The classes

1. **Market statistics, valuations, and forecasts.** Market sizes, growth projections, funding/valuation figures for named companies (e.g., a post's "Cursor raised at a $29.3 billion valuation"), adoption percentages without a named primary study. Sheets settle what protocols and vendors *did*; they do not settle market numbers.
2. **Predictions and timelines.** "UCP will be ubiquitous in six to twelve months," vendor roadmaps beyond dated announcements, any future-tense claim about unlaunched features (except where a sheet itself records "announced, no date" — the *absence* of a date is judgeable; the prediction is not).
3. **Third parties' internal decisions and motives.** Why a company dropped or adopted something internally (e.g., "Perplexity dropped MCP internally"), bank or network strategy intent beyond their published statements, negotiations not on the public record.
4. **Hands-on test anecdotes.** First-person endpoint probes and screenshots (e.g., a post's claim that Target/Walmart/Best Buy/Etsy UCP endpoints returned 404). A wave cannot re-run the author's test rig; the claim stands or falls as testimony, not fact.
5. **Partner and endorsement counts without a roster.** "20+ endorsers," "60+ partners" as bare numbers — judgeable only where a sheet carries the named roster or the primary announcement itself states the count in a dated context.
6. **Competitor characterizations.** Comparative quality claims ("X's checkout is broken," "Y is abandoning Z") — positioning dressed as fact. Vendor positioning quoted *as* positioning is judgeable (Sheets 4–5 mark it); the truth of the positioning is not.
7. **Legal and regulatory outcomes not yet in force.** Liability allocations, dispute outcomes, and regulatory effects that have not happened. Exception: the *terms text* of an instrument in force is judgeable (e.g., Wells Fargo's fence language effective Nov 24, 2026 — the words are judgeable, the outcomes are not; Visa's CE 3.0 clause, Sheet 5).
8. **Vendor volume and adoption claims without a named tracker.** Transaction counts, GMV, "millions of agents" — judgeable only with the tracker and date attached (Sheet 6, item 20 shows why: x402's trackers disagree by an order of magnitude).
9. **Boilerplate definitional FAQ blocks.** The repeated definitional FAQ text that recurs across the site's posts: even where a definition happens to be right, it is template text, not a sourced claim; waves treat it as copy to be consolidated, not facts to be verified line by line.
10. **Anything sourced only to the site's own earlier posts.** Circular citation — a claim whose only support is another page on the same site. Circular support is not support.

## How waves use this file
- A claim matching a class above → **NEEDS-VERIFY**, with the class number recorded. No contributor call is spent arguing with it.
- A claim outside these classes that no sheet covers → also NEEDS-VERIFY, and the gap is logged as a candidate sheet amendment (Phase 0b re-earn, per the programme's 14-day half-life rule).
- NEEDS-VERIFY is a routing verdict, not a quality verdict: it sends the claim to Will's tap queue or the consolidation map, never to silent deletion.
