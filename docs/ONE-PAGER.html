Tygart-OS on Meta Muse: The Fact-Gated Fleet

Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon · Startup Track · Tygart Media — Will Tygart, with Glint (his AI chief of staff)

The problem

Publishing at machine speed breaks facts quietly. A single AI editing pass invents plausible, confident, wrong "fixes" — and no human can hand-check thousands of live pages. Our six-site fleet (4,415 pages) needed an editor that works at fleet scale without ever grading its own homework.

What we built — two systems on Meta's Muse models

1. The Trio. A three-pass pipeline on muse-spark-1.3: a Scout (low effort) flags suspect claims → a Fixer (medium effort) drafts corrections → an Adjudicator (max effort) acts as a pure fact gate, striking anything it cannot ground verbatim in the source. The separation is the invention: the model that proposes never approves.

2. The Hopper + translator routers. Everything incoming — recordings, documents, links — lands in one dumb intake box. A per-target router learns each destination's template, routes the item, and reads back confirmation; exceptions surface in one place instead of vanishing.

What it did — real production, Oct 26 – Nov 2, on the hackathon's credits

  • 4,415 published pages across 6 sites read for monetization fit → 2,769 strong product candidates — the candidate layer for the free / $7 / $17 download ladder now shipping across the fleet.
  • Full-site fact sweeps: Continuity Hub, 93 pages → 647 adjudicated fixes; Risk Coverage Hub, 201 pages → 1,415 fixes; converted into 36 staged work orders for human-tapped execution.
  • 174 working-session recordings mined: 3,093 window reads across three reading pools; 245 transcription calls, 138,144 words, $3.20 total.
  • Consensus miner: 339 findings certified by verbatim quote validation against sources — 4.8% of proposals dropped by the validation, on purpose.

What we learned — feedback to Meta

  • The medium-effort fixer hallucinates fixes fluently. Only the max-effort gate makes output shippable — effort tiering is a trust architecture, not just a cost knob.
  • Medium is the sweet spot for routing decisions; past ~3k reasoning tokens you buy strictness, not facts.
  • Wonder-as-judge failed its trial and was dropped. Structured feedback filed in FEEDBACK-TO-META.md.
