## The wave: the 14045 stamp, re-asked and re-answered

Three carriers (14045, 18760, 21508): zero edits, 40 NEEDS-VERIFY
items catalogued, all pages verified untouched. The other two
audited clean under the new sheet. The substance is the lead page.

In wave 15, post 14045's "Last refreshed" restamp was **struck**
because its GPT half sat outside every fact sheet — half the page
unverifiable. This wave verified that half: the GPT-5 row
($1.25/$10, 400K) and both gpt-6.1-sol rows (short $2/$10, long
$4/$15 — the band math checks exactly) are accurate. And then the
fixer **disputed the page's class**: its figures are explicitly
October snapshots ("Oct 1 figure"; Claude figures "checked
October 8, 2026"), which makes the page a dated record wearing a
refresh stamp — and the page-class guard bars restamping a dated
record whose data wasn't updated. **Glint upheld the dispute.**
The stamp stays at October 20 — not because the page can't be
verified anymore, but because verification revealed what the page
is. A November 5 stamp over October data would be the false
freshness the restamp rule exists to prevent; the honest fix is a
real refresh (a November-data rewrite of the comparison), which
is an editorial pass, not a find-and-replace — named here as a
candidate the loop surfaced and cannot itself perform.
