## The sheet

Built the slow way, on purpose: primary public sources, state by
state — statute text read verbatim (Idaho Code §§55-2504/2509,
Va. Code §32.1-229.01, Minn. Stat. §§144.496/144.4961, Fla. Stat.
§404.056, ORC Ch. 3723, N.D.C.C. Ch. 47-10, MCA §§75-3-601–607,
CGS §§10-220/20-327b, Colorado's HB21-1195 and SB23-206),
official program pages (IDHW, NJDEP, MDH, CT DPH), the EPA zone
table, and — labeled as what it is — the AARST/NRPP
state-requirements compilation where a statute read wasn't
practical. Every entry carries its source class on the sheet:
[T1] read directly, [CONV] compilation convergence,
[BOUNDED-ABSENCE] no such rule found in the state's materials,
ABSENT — not settled, stays open. One research catch deserves
its own line: a content-farm page describes an elaborate Idaho
"Radon Professional License" — biennial renewal, CE hours, a
licensing program — **that does not exist**. Idaho's own health
department recommends national certification and runs no such
program. The sheet records the fabrication so the loop never
has to re-litigate it.

## What jurisdiction-by-research costs — the scaling verdict

A vendor sheet costs one pricing page and one catalog call.
This sheet cost roughly **twenty-five primary-source reads
across nine research passes** — the entire first phase of the
wave, before a single contributor call fired — to cover one
cluster's catalogue. It produced 9 real corrections, so the
cost bought something. But the economics are the verdict:
jurisdiction-by-research scales only where the catalogue is
bounded in advance and the pages are many (this one sheet
serves a 23-page cluster). As a general expansion method —
new territory, research a sheet, audit — the research phase,
not the contributor tokens, is the budget. The loop's cheap
jurisdictions were the vendors'; its expensive ones will be
chosen, not wandered into.

Usage: 22 calls, 234,686 tokens (10 scouts 102,005; 10 fixers
104,780; 2 ritual re-sends 27,901 — all counted).
Credit-equivalent ≈ $0.035. Card $0 — free credits; no cliff
signals. Pool per Will: $35.34 remaining.
